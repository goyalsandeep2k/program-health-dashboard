🔄 Program Operating Rhythm — Opmech Cadence
🏛️ Project Helix Steerco
Bi-Weekly · Wk 1 & 3
Executive alignment, cross-org escalation resolution, program-level go/no-go decisions. The highest accountability forum for the program — input goals, delivery status, and blockers that need executive action.
Exec Sponsors Biz Leadership VEP Leadership GTM-T Leadership Program Driver
📋 Program Meeting
Bi-Weekly · Wk 1 & 3
Cross-pillar program status review. Risks, dependencies, decisions needed, and milestone tracking across all 5 pillars. The connecting layer between workstream execution and Steerco escalation.
  • Lead Management
  • Seller Experiences
  • Deal & Order Desk
  • AI for Sales
  • Foundations

Weekly
Deep technical alignment on platform decisions, architecture trade-offs, and cross-team engineering dependencies. Prep (T-7) ensures agenda is pre-aligned and decisions are unblocked in the session itself.
