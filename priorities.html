🧭 North Star — How This List Works
Every program is scored across 5 criteria and ranked by Revenue Impact — the primary decision variable for staffing and project prioritization. Items at the same revenue tier are broken by Priority Score. Platform investments carry a 20% eng capacity floor regardless of rank — they enable future revenue even when their current score is lower. This list is the single source of truth for what gets staffed, what gets deferred, and what ships first.
