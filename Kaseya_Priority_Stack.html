🧭 Strategic Intent — How Kaseya Wins in FY26
Kaseya's competitive strategy centers on reducing tool sprawl and administrative labor costs for MSPs — achieving this through the Kaseya 365 all-inclusive bundle, AI automation across the RMM/PSA stack, and a unified cyber resilience platform. Programs are ranked by MSP Business Impact: direct ARR growth from bundle adoption, cost savings unlocked for MSP partners, and revenue unblocked by compliance certifications. All three pillars carry dedicated engineering capacity floors with no program deprioritized below minimum viable staffing.
